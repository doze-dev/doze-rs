Slow a topic

Delay the answer to one topic's requests, for a while. Your producers and consumers see slow, not broken.

Which requests
Produce delays the answer to a write, so the producer waits for its ack. A producer with acks=0 does not wait, and sees nothing.
Topic
orders[[g:chev-d 14]]
3 partitions. Written by orders and admin-cli, read by payments-workers. Replaces any rule on the same topic and API.
Delay
ms
Added to the normal time. A request that took 3 ms is answered after about 403 ms. The wire shows the total.
Share of requests
1 in 4[[g:chev-d 14]]
Every 4th matching request is slowed. Choose every request to slow them all.
Ends after
Every rule ends by itself. The longest is 30 minutes. You can clear it earlier.
CancelStart rehearsal
[[g:code 14]]The rule it makes
doze:SlowTopic
  api:    Produce
  topic:  orders
  delay:  400ms
  share:  1 in 4
  ttl:    10m

What the wire will show

A slowed request, tagged, with its real time.

22:14:08.350Produce v9 orders-1[[g:flask 12]]lab405 ms

A request the rule leaves alone is untagged and as fast as before.
