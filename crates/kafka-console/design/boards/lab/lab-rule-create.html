New rehearsal

A rule bends requests to one topic for a few minutes, then ends. Choose how.

Slow a topic
Add delay to Produce or Fetch. Nothing fails; everything takes longer.
Fail some requests
Answer 1 in N with an error Kafka has, such as REQUEST_TIMED_OUT.

Dropping a connection, expiring a member, forcing a rebalance and running the cleaner do not need a rule. They happen once and are on the page behind this one.
