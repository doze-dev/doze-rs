Only what production code can honestly cause. The first two are rules that end on their own; the others happen once, and leave a record of what they did.

[[g:clock 18 d-compute]]Slow a topicrule
Add delay to Produce or Fetch for one topic, for minutes. See which of your apps feels it first.
[[g:warn 18 d-compute]]Fail some requestsrule
Answer 1 in N requests to a topic with an error Kafka has, such as REQUEST_TIMED_OUT.
[[g:link 18 d-compute]]Drop a connectiononce
Close one app's connections and see whether it reconnects on its own.
[[g:clock 18 d-compute]]Expire a member's sessiononce
Remove a group member as a missed heartbeat would, and read the story it leaves.
[[g:refresh 18 d-compute]]Force a rebalanceonce
Start a rebalance of one group without waiting for a reason.
[[g:log 18 d-compute]]Run the cleaner nowonce
Clean one compacted topic this minute instead of at the next look, every 15 s.
