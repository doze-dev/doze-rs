Fail some requests

Answer a share of one topic's requests with an error Kafka has, and see whether your client retries, waits or gives up.

Which requests
Topic
inventory.levels[[g:chev-d 14]]
1 partition, compacted. Written by catalog, read by search-indexer.
Share of requests
1 in 5[[g:chev-d 14]]
Answer with
REQUEST_TIMED_OUT (7)
The broker took too long. Clients retry; most treat it as a normal hiccup.
NOT_LEADER_OR_FOLLOWER (6)
This broker is not the partition's leader. A client refreshes its metadata and asks again.
UNKNOWN_TOPIC_OR_PARTITION (3)
The topic looks missing. Some clients stop; some create it if they may.
Failed, not Refused. No rule of Kafka's refused the request. The lab answered it, so the wire says Failed and carries the lab tag.
