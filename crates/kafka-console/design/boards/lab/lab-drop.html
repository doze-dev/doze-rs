Drop a connection

Close the connections of one app, as a network blip would. The broker keeps all its data; the app has to notice and come back.

App (client.id)
catalog[[g:chev-d 14]]
2 connections from 127.0.0.1 now. It writes inventory.levels.
How long
Once closes what is open and lets it reconnect. Keep dropping is a rule: each new connection from catalog is closed, until it ends. Once leaves no rule behind, only a line under Ended.
[[g:code 14]]What it does
doze:DropConnection client=catalog once
CancelDrop connection
