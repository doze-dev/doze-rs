If it is not ended

At 22:14:33, 19 s from now, the broker aborts it, whatever the producer is doing. It writes an abort marker to orders-0 (at 16,104) and orders-1 (at 16,051), so the last stable offset moves up to the high watermark, and it fences producer 4017: the epoch goes from 2 to 3, and a Produce at epoch 2 is Refused INVALID_PRODUCER_EPOCH (47). A well-behaved client asks InitProducerId again and starts a new transaction; read_committed consumers skip the 7 records of the aborted one.
