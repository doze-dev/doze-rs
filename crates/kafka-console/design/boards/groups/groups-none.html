Groups are made by consumers, not here

A group exists once a consumer joins with a group.id, or an app commits an offset for one. Start a consumer and it shows up here with its members and lag in a few seconds.

# read orders as the group "payments-workers" kcat -C -b 127.0.0.1:9092 -G payments-workers orders
Go to Topics

Nothing to refresh by hand. An empty group with no committed offsets is forgotten by the broker at its next sweep and leaves this list.
