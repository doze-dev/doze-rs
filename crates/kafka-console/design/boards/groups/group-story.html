Where the cause comes from

New

Kafka's protocol has no call that says why a group rebalanced; a client sees only that it must rejoin. The broker knows, because it is the one deciding. It records the cause at that moment, with the member and how long the rebalance took.

The causes it can name
Member joineda new member, or the first of an empty group
Member leftLeaveGroup, sent by the client
Session expiredno heartbeat within the session timeout
Topic changedpartitions added to a subscribed topic
Rejoined with new protocolsa member changed what it offers
Rebalance timeoutmembers did not rejoin in time

Is it flapping?

Not by the rule: one session expiry in 6 h, and a clean leave never counts. fulfilment-py-2 did join and leave within 5 m 17 s; worth a look if it repeats.
