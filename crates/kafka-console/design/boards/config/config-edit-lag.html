What it is how long a record waits before it may be cleaned (min), and how long before it must be (max)
Now min 0 set on this topic; max not set (no limit)
Allowed min at least 0, max at least 1, and min not above max
Takes effect at the cleaner's next look, within 15 s
Here with min 2 h a record is left alone for 2 h; with max 1 h it would be forced after 1 h
