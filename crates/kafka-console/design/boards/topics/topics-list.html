One internal topic is hidden. Turn on Show internal and this row joins the list:
__consumer_offsets internalread only · the group coordinator's name for where commits live50compactnot a lognone

Why it is read only. __consumer_offsets is Kafka's own name for the place the group coordinator keeps each group's committed offsets, spread over 50 partitions. This broker keeps commits in its own store and answers Kafka's offset calls from it, so there is no log here to read, produce to, delete from, or change. The name is shown because Kafka clients and tools know it; the 50 partitions decide which coordinator slot a group lands in. Look at a group's commits on its Lag tab instead: open Groups.

The filter matches names as you type: when nothing matches. A broker with no topics yet shows this instead.
