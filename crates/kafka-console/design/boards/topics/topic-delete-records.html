Kafka says the log start moves to this offset; earlier records are no longer served.
Range 0 up to the high watermark (16,104). -1 means the high watermark: delete everything.
Takes effect at once, kept through a restart. Below or at the start changes nothing; past the high watermark is OFFSET_OUT_OF_RANGE.
Here it cannot be undone, and the last stable offset never falls below the new start.

On a topic that only compacts this is refused: see POLICY_VIOLATION.
