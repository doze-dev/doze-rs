What restarting does

An offline partition stays offline until restart. Restart the machine, not only doze.

After a failed flush the kernel may mark the lost pages clean. A new doze process on the same machine can find them in the page cache, and its flush then succeeds without writing anything. Only starting the machine again clears that.

Records acknowledged with acks -1 were flushed before the failure. With acks 1 the last 500 ms before it may not have been.
