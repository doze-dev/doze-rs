Access

OpenNew

Anyone who can reach 127.0.0.1:9092 may read, write and change anything: nobody signs in, no ACL exists and the default allows. This page shows each of those three switches and what it holds.

ACLs

What DescribeAcls returns, read only. Even with no sign-in, ACLs bind User:ANONYMOUS, so a broker that has them is not open to everything.

No ACLs

With --acl-default allow nothing is refused for lack of one. The first ACL on a topic turns the default off for that topic and refuses everyone it does not name.

The console

It reads and writes the broker the way a client does, as one more principal, and it has no login of its own.
