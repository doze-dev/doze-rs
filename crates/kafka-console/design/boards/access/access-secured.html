Access

Sign-in requiredNew

Clients sign in with SCRAM over TLS, and ACLs decide what each one may do. Everything below is read from the broker; no password, key or salt is ever shown.

[[g:info 18]]
A different instance from the rest of this canvas. Every other board is the open broker. This one is the same page for a broker started with --sasl-mechanisms SCRAM-SHA-256,SCRAM-SHA-512 --acl-default deny --acl-super-user User:admin --tls-cert ~/larder/tls/broker.pem; the top bar chip says so.
