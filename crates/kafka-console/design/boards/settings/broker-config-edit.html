Edit log.retention.ms

How long a topic's segments are kept before retention may delete them.

Config
log.retention.ms[[g:chev-d 14]]
One of the five. Another, such as num.partitions, cannot be changed while the broker runs: what Kafka says.
Applies to
This broker's own value wins over the one for all brokers.
New value
ms = 3 d
Now: -1, no limit. Source: static broker, from the flag --retention-ms 0.
Allowed: -1 or any larger number of milliseconds. -1 keeps segments for ever.
Takes effect: at the next retention pass, every 5 m. No restart; the change is kept on disk.
Who sees it: the topics with no retention.ms of their own: payments.settled, pick-lists, pick-lists.dlq, orders.dlq. orders (7 d), notifications.email (1 d) and clickstream (1 h) keep theirs. inventory.levels and customer.profiles are compact only: retention does not apply.
[[g:code 14]]The call
IncrementalAlterConfigs v1
  BROKER "" (all brokers)
  SET log.retention.ms = 259200000
