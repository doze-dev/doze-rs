Waiting for your first request

Listening

The moment a client sends its first request, this card says who it is: its client.id, the software and the versions it speaks. Three steps get one through, each a command you can paste.

1
Ask the broker what it has
kcat -b 127.0.0.1:9092 -L
2
Send a message (auto-create makes the topic, 1 partition)
echo "hello" | kcat -P -b 127.0.0.1:9092 -t orders -X client.id=admin-cli
3
Watch it arrive
Open Live: the request is the first row on the wire, with its caller, topic and result.
