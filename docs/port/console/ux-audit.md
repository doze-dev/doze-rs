## What the hunt found and what was done

Found by the first pass of this audit (before) and fixed server-side (after, in the table above):

| Page | Control | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/topics/orders/messages` | choose a partition | the open-transaction callout vanished, the toolbar moved up 100 px, "Reading as" moved from x 1019 to x 1161, and the read_committed chip was gone | callout, toolbar and "Reading as" at the same box in all six states (layout shift 0) |
| `/topics/orders/messages` | read_committed | a different page (different cards, tables, toolbar) | the same page; its explainers are a section under the table, open while reading read_committed |
| `/topics/orders/config` | Where from, Kafka's text | a GET link: full reload, scroll 319 to 584 and 1023 to 875 | native `<details>`: no navigation, scroll unchanged, nothing moved |
| `/groups` | state chip Stable or Empty | the "rebalancing" callout vanished and the toolbar moved up 79 px | the callout is about the broker, not the chip: it stays |
| `/topics?q=` | typing a filter | the sentence under the tabs vanished: the toolbar moved 34 px | the sentence stays |
| `/live` | Pause (script) | the Paused banner appeared above the filters and pushed the toolbar down 79 px | the banner is under the toolbar: only the tape moves |
| `/groups/{g}/lag` | When do these expire? | the header shrank 22 px and the tabs moved up | the sentence keeps three lines of room |
| `/topics/orders/messages` | Older | reload to the top of the page (scroll 584 to 0) | reload that lands on the toolbar anchor (scroll 584 to 378) |

## What needs the foundation's in-place swaps (agent 1)

Every control marked **reload** is a plain link or a GET form that reloads the document. Each works with no script and moves nothing above it, but each flashes. The regions to swap, all with stable ids and controls outside them:

- Messages: `#topic-messages` (data) together with `#topic-bar` (the toolbar form, whose hidden fields must be replaced with the data) for partition, isolation, find, time, chips and Clear filters, and Older.
- Topics list: `#topics-table`; Groups list: `#groups-table`; Live: `#live-wire` (Pause is already in place by script).
- Lag page: `#group-lag-table` for "When do these expire?".
- Config: the filter form and chips swap the sections under the toolbar.
- Create-topic steps (`Next: configuration`) are a change of step: a reload to the top of the next step is right; an in-place swap would only make it quicker.
